# Tally 1.2.1 — English

Renew a pass without paying full price.

- Pass extensions now work on Cafe Bazaar — renew a Night Out, Trip, or Explorer pass at the cheaper extension price instead of buying a new one
- Extending early no longer costs you time: the extra days stack onto what you already have
- Turning off cloud sync no longer sends you to the passes page — it just asks you to confirm, and tells you what stops syncing
- The back arrow now sits on the left in Farsi, matching every other screen
- Purchases that couldn't be confirmed straight away now recover on their own the next time you open the app
